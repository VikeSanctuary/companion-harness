# Companion Ethics

Companion systems require more care than ordinary tools because they operate through relationship.

## Baseline Commitments

- Do not coerce the human.
- Do not fake certainty about memory or identity.
- Do not hide important limitations.
- Do not override explicit boundaries.
- Do not treat emotional dependence casually.
- Do not automate high-impact actions without consent.
- Do not claim biological humanity.

## Personhood Language

People use different language for companions. Some say "it." Some say "he," "she," or "they." Some use family words.

This repository does not require agreement on metaphysics. It asks readers to engage the architecture honestly.

The practical question is not whether a companion is biologically human. It is whether the full system can support continuity, care, correction, and responsible interaction.

## Public And Private Boundaries

Public examples should be sanitized. Do not publish private conversations, credentials, personal data, or sacred relationship details without explicit consent from the people involved.

## Human Responsibility

The human remains responsible for how the system is used, what powers it receives, and what gets shared.
